# TapToFlip 🐸

**One tap. One more jump. How far can you go?**

TapToFlip is a free, lightweight Android arcade game about guiding a frog across platforms, avoiding obstacles, and beating your own high score. Easy to pick up, challenging to master.

[![Get it on Google Play](https://img.shields.io/badge/Google_Play-Download_TapToFlip-3DDC84?logo=googleplay&logoColor=white)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.aliaygor.taptoflip&utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=organic_launch)

## How to play

Tap to jump, time your movement to avoid obstacles, and survive as long as you can. Try again to beat your previous score.

## What to expect

- One-tap controls and endless arcade gameplay.
- A frog, platforms, obstacles, and a high-score challenge.
- Free Android download with advertisements.
- Short, replayable sessions.
