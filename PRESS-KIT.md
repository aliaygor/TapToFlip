## Short description

Guide a frog across platforms in a simple, endlessly replayable arcade challenge. Tap to jump, avoid obstacles, stay alive, and beat your high score.

## About the game

TapToFlip is a lightweight Android arcade game built around one-tap controls. Players guide a frog across platforms while avoiding obstacles and trying to survive as long as possible. The goal is to beat your previous high score, making it suitable for quick, replayable sessions. The game is free to download and contains advertisements.

## Gameplay

- Tap to jump.
- Avoid obstacles and stay alive.
- Keep progressing and try to beat your high score.
